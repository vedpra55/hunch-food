Meet Hunch

A living drop of flavour that orders for you.

Hunch has no face to fake. It feels through its eyes, its shape, its colour and its glow. It sits on top of every screen, and everything you see grows out of it.

Tap a mood. Move your pointer over Hunch and it looks at you.

Every stage has an identity

Six moments, one character

Each stage owns a colour pool and a Hunch mood. There is only ever one Hunch on screen: in tracking it leaves the island and rides the route. Plates are generated placeholders; production uses real top-down photography cut to a circle.

Motion

Everything is born from Hunch

Hunch thinks out loud in short chips, then the answer grows out of it: scale from Hunch's position, blur 8 px to sharp, rise 16 px. Springs everywhere, so any motion can be interrupted.

spring.snappystiffness 420 · damping 32. Taps, chips, toggles.
spring.gentlestiffness 200 · damping 28. Sheets and cards settling. The only spring in checkout and support.
spring.bouncystiffness 320 · damping 14. Found it, offer accepted.
dur.micro120 ms. Press states.
dur.base240 ms. Fades and colour shifts between moods.
dur.emerge420 ms. Generated UI arriving.
stagger40 ms between chips and carousel plates.
breath4 s cycle. Only Hunch and the primary action breathe.
reducedBlink and colour stay. Wobble, travel and bounce go. Emergence becomes a 200 ms fade.
Colour

A warm canvas, so the food carries the colour

Masala belongs to Hunch and the main action. Each flavour owns one part of the journey. Groups are made with soft colour pools and food-tinted shadows, never with borders.
