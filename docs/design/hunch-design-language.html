Foundations · v0.2

Calm interface. One lively character.

The interface is neutral and quiet. Hunch is the only thing that moves on its own, and the only thing that carries colour. Emotion comes from its face, not from more colours.

Pick a mood, or move your pointer and Hunch watches it. Tap Hunch to make it happy.

01 · Character

Nine moods, one colour

A soft persimmon pearl with an ink face. The lids carry most of the emotion. Care and Sleepy fade slightly toward stone; no mood changes hue.

BodyLight single-hue orb, pearl highlight, inner glow. Breathes, hops, leans.
Eyes and lidsInk ovals with a catchlight. Lid height and angle set the mood.
MouthSmile, grin, "o", flat or soft. Small, and changes with the moment.
Blush and auraPersimmon 300 blush when pleased. A faint glow, which becomes rings when listening.
